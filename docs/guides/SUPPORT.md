# AXIS Support & Assistance Guide

Welcome to the AXIS support documentation. This guide details how to resolve issues, generate diagnostic data, and seek help.

---

## 1. Primary Channels

- **GitHub Issues**: For bug reports and tweak audit requests: [GitHub Issues](../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE)
- **GitHub Discussions**: For optimization advice, hardware tuning, and Q&A.

---

## 2. Generating Diagnostics for Support

When opening a support ticket or seeking assistance:
1. Open **AXIS**.
2. Navigate to the **Settings** tab (or press Ctrl+K and search).
3. Under **Diagnostic Report**, click **Copy report to clipboard**.
4. Paste the report into your issue ticket.

---

## 3. Safe Recovery & System Protection

If any optimization needs to be reverted:
- **Individual Revert**: Click on any tweak card and choose **Revert to default**.
- **Global Revert**: Open Settings and click **Export Snapshot** or restore from a Windows Restore Point.
